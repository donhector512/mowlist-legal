MowList Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Draft. Not legal advice. Have a lawyer review before you publish.

MowList is an app that helps small lawn-care businesses run their route, keep track of customers, write customer texts, and get paid. MowList is run by Hector Vasquez, an individual app developer in Texas, USA ("MowList," "we," "us").

This policy explains, in plain language, what information MowList handles, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It covers the MowList apps for iPhone (and Android, when available), our quote, payment, and lead web pages, and our backend service.

The short version

  • We collect only what the app needs to work.
  • Most of your data stays on your phone. We keep a copy on our servers only for features that need it: quote and pay links, payments, crew sync, and photos.
  • We never sell your data or your customers' data. We do not show ads, and we do not share data for advertising or track you across other apps or websites.
  • Stripe handles card and bank details. MowList never sees or stores card or bank account numbers.
  • Texts to your customers are sent from your own phone through its Messages app. MowList writes the message and you tap Send. MowList doesn't send or receive text messages on its servers.
  • You can delete your data at any time with Settings > Delete my account.

1. Who this policy is about

  • Business users: lawn-care operators who use the MowList app, plus crew members they invite.
  • Homeowners: the customers of a business user. A homeowner's information gets into MowList when the business adds it, or when the homeowner fills in a form, opens a quote link, or pays through a link.

Important for homeowners: your lawn-care business decides what information to keep about you and how to use it. For that information, the business is in charge (it is the "controller"). MowList is the software provider that stores and processes it for the business (we are a "service provider" or "processor"). See Section 6.

2.3 Collected automatically

  • Web pages and server logs: When someone opens a MowList link or the app talks to our server, our systems record basic technical information (IP address, browser or device type, time, page requested). We use it to keep the service running and secure, and we keep it for a short time (see Section 7). We try to keep phone numbers and full addresses out of our logs.
  • Cookies: Our link pages use only cookies or similar storage that the page needs to work (for example, to remember language or keep a payment session going). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Stripe's payment page may set its own cookies to prevent fraud.
  • No analytics or ad SDKs: The app has no advertising SDKs and no third-party tracking. If you chose to share analytics with Apple or Google, they may send us anonymous crash and usage reports under their own policies.

2.4 Location

  • MowList can use your phone's location (only if you allow it) for "Use my location" and "Start from here," and to put your stops in a good driving order.
  • Location is used on your device. We do not keep a history of where you go, and we do not track you in the background.
  • Customer addresses may be turned into map points using your phone's built-in map service (Apple Maps / MapKit on iPhone).
  • When you tap Go, MowList opens the map app you picked (Google Maps, Apple Maps, or Waze) and sends it the destination address. That app handles the address under its own privacy policy.
  • You can turn off location in your phone's settings. The app still works, but you'll need to type addresses.

2.5 What we don't collect

  • Card numbers, bank account numbers, or Social Security numbers (Stripe handles these).
  • Your phone's contacts list. MowList does not ask to read your contacts.
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA). We never ask to track you.

3. Why we use it

We use information only to:

  1. Run the app: show your route, customers, and money; sync between your devices and your crew; back up photos.
  2. Prepare texts for the business to send: MowList fills in a message ("on the way," "done," bills, quotes, reminders, rain-day notices) in the customer's language and opens the Messages app on the business user's phone. The business user reviews it and taps Send.
  3. Make quote, payment, and lead links work, and process payments through Stripe.
  4. Keep things safe: prevent fraud, spam, and abuse; respect customers' "no texts" settings; fix bugs; secure our systems.
  5. Support you when you contact us.
  6. Follow the law: taxes, texting rules, legal requests, and protecting rights.
  7. Tell you about the service: important notices like changes to these terms, security alerts, or billing problems. We won't send marketing texts to homeowners. We do not send marketing emails to business users; if we ever do, every email will have an unsubscribe link.

We do not use homeowner data for our own marketing, and we do not combine it across businesses to build profiles.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not "share" it for targeted advertising (as those words are used in US state privacy laws). We don't sell or share precise location or any other sensitive data.

5. Text messages

  • How texts work: MowList writes a text for the business (in the customer's language) and opens the Messages app on the business user's own phone. The business user reviews it and taps Send. The text comes from the business's phone number, through Apple and the business's mobile carrier. MowList's servers don't send, receive, or store text messages, and we can't see replies.
  • Texts are about service and billing only: on the way, done, bills, quotes, reminders, and rain or skip notices. No marketing.
  • The business must have the homeowner's permission before texting them. The app asks the business to confirm this when adding a customer.
  • To stop texts, homeowners can reply to the business (for example, "STOP") or tell the business in person. The business must then turn on "No texts" for that customer in MowList, and the app will stop preparing texts for them.
  • Message and data rates from your carrier may apply. Delivery depends on Apple and the carriers.
  • We do not share phone numbers or texting consent with anyone for their marketing.

6. Homeowner data: the business is in charge

  • The business user decides what to record about its customers and is responsible for having a right to use it, including texting consent.
  • MowList processes that information only to provide the service to that business, following the business's instructions and this policy.
  • Homeowners: to see, fix, or delete your information, please contact your lawn-care business first. You can also email us at mowlistapp@gmail.com. We'll pass your request to the business and help where we can. To stop texts, tell the business. Texts come from its phone, not from MowList.

Delete your account

In the app. Open MowList, go to Settings, and tap Delete my account. This deletes MowList data on that device and asks our server to delete your account, customers, photos, and links. Server deletion is done within 30 days (backups roll off within another 30 days). Texts you already sent stay in your phone's Messages app. Delete them there if you want. Quote and pay links stop working after deletion. Deleting your account does not close your Stripe account (close it through Stripe) and does not cancel an Apple or Google subscription (cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings). Your phone may also keep MowList data in iCloud or Google backups that you control. To remove those, delete them in your phone's iCloud or Google settings.

Homeowners. To ask that your information be deleted, email mowlistapp@gmail.com. We'll pass your request to the lawn-care business that keeps it and help where we can. You can also contact that business first. To stop texts, tell the business. Texts come from its phone, not from MowList.

More on how deletion works is in Section 8.

8. Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings > Delete my account. This deletes MowList data on that device and asks our server to delete your account, customers, photos, and links. Server deletion is done within 30 days (backups roll off within another 30 days). Texts you already sent stay in your phone's Messages app. Delete them there if you want.
  • iCloud or Google backups: your phone may also keep MowList data in backups that you control. To remove those, delete them in your phone's iCloud or Google settings.
  • Stripe: deleting MowList data doesn't close your Stripe account. Close it through Stripe.
  • Subscriptions: deleting data doesn't cancel an Apple or Google subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.
  • Quote and pay links stop working after deletion.

9. Security

  • All data between the app, our web pages, and our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Server data and backups are encrypted at rest. On iPhone, MowList uses Apple's built-in data protection.
  • Card data goes only to Stripe, which is certified to PCI DSS Level 1.
  • Quote and pay links use long, random codes that expire.
  • Only the people who need access to run MowList have it, and we keep secret keys out of the app.

No system is 100% secure. If you think your account or data is at risk, email mowlistapp@gmail.com right away.

10. If there's a data breach

If we learn that personal information was taken or seen by someone who shouldn't have it, we will:

  • notify affected people without unreasonable delay, and no later than 60 days after we determine a breach happened, as Texas law requires (Tex. Bus. & Com. Code §521.053) and as other states' laws require;
  • notify the Texas Attorney General within 30 days if 250 or more Texans are affected;
  • tell affected business users right away so they can inform their customers, and help them do it.

11. Your privacy rights

Wherever you live in the US, you can ask us to:

  • see the personal information we hold about you, and get a copy (the app also has export tools);
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • confirm we don't sell it or use it for targeted ads (we don't).

Email mowlistapp@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request." We may need to confirm who you are (for example, by a code sent to your phone or email). We'll answer within 45 days (sometimes longer, if the law allows and we tell you why). If we say no, you can ask us to reconsider by replying to our answer, and if you still disagree you can contact your state attorney general. We won't treat you differently for using these rights. Someone you authorize can make a request for you.

Homeowners: see Section 6. Requests about data a business keeps may need to go to that business.

Global Privacy Control / Do Not Track: we don't track or sell data, so there's nothing to opt out of. We honor these signals anyway.

12. Children

MowList is a business tool for adults. It is not meant for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Business users must be at least 18. If you think a child has given us information, email mowlistapp@gmail.com and we'll delete it.

13. Where data is stored

MowList is for US businesses. Our servers and providers are in the United States.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We'll change the "Last updated" date at the top. If a change is important, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect. We will not use data already collected in a materially different way without your consent.

If the English and Spanish versions differ, the English version controls, unless the law requires otherwise.
